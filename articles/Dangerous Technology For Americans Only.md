# Dangerous Technology For Americans Only

> [!quote] Summary
> AI nationalism, safety and European weakness.

## Analysis & TLDR
- **TLDR:**
  - The US is leveraging AI export controls to enforce nationalistic boundaries on technology access.
  - This action highlights Europe's severe dependency on American tech infrastructure.
  - Europe's reflex to regulate rather than innovate creates structural weakness.
  - The US model succeeds in capability but fails in societal cohesion.
  - True progress requires open-source collaboration over geopolitical supremacy.
- **The Good:**
  - Directly attacks Europe's self-imposed bureaucratic stagnation.
  - Recognizes open-source as a structural defense against centralized corporate hegemony.
- **The Less Good:**
  - Idealizes international cooperation while dismissing the realpolitik of hostile state actors.
  - Fails to propose concrete steps beyond "more ambition" and "deregulation".
