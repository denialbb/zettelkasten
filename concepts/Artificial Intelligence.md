Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a broad field of computer science focused on creating machines capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.

## Subfields

- [[Machine Learning]] — algorithms that improve through experience
- [[Reinforcement Learning]] — learning through interaction and reward
- [[Computer Vision]] — interpreting visual information
- [[Neural Network]] — computational models inspired by biological neural networks
- Natural Language Processing
- Expert Systems
- Robotics

## Types of AI

- **Narrow AI** (Weak AI) — designed for a specific task
- **General AI** (Strong AI) — theoretical machine with general intelligence
- **Artificial Superintelligence** — hypothetical AI surpassing human intelligence
